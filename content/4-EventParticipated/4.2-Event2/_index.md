## Event Objectives

**FCAJ - Agentic AI Build Week** was a showcase and sharing event for projects built during Agentic AI Build Week. The event gave participants the opportunity to observe teams presenting ideas, demonstrating products, and sharing how Agentic AI can be applied to practical problems.

Through the pitching sessions, I gained a clearer view of how an AI idea can be developed into a working prototype and how teams present the problem, solution, architecture, and product value. The event also helped me better understand the Agentic AI trend, where AI does not only answer questions but can also participate in task workflows, support decision-making, and automate parts of work.

## Shared Projects

**The winning team** presented a project that addressed the inconvenience of the current fast-food ordering process. Instead of requiring users to go through multiple intermediate steps or platforms, the team proposed integrating the ordering flow directly into Zalo, allowing customers to order McDonald's from an application they already use frequently. This idea helps shorten the ordering journey, reduce user friction, and demonstrate how Agentic AI can be embedded into familiar communication channels to support real-world commerce workflows.

**Signal Scout** focused on detecting early strategic changes in companies. The system connects scattered signals into a clearer analytical story, helping strategy, risk management, and competitive intelligence teams make evidence-based decisions.

**Solution Architect Professional Native App** supported Solution Architects during requirement analysis and architecture design. The application can understand project requirements in natural language, draft high-level cloud architecture options, generate editable diagrams, and provide initial AWS cost estimates.

**S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D.** by team 3KA used computer vision and agentic AI to analyze human flow in real time. The system can detect crowd density, monitor queue conditions, predict overcrowding risks, and recommend actions for operators.
