## Event Objectives

**FCAJ Community Day - June 2026** is a community event organized to connect people interested in **AWS Cloud**, **Artificial Intelligence**, **DevOps**, **AI Agents**, and modern technology trends. The event helped me gain a more practical view of how AI and Cloud are being applied in enterprises, especially in system operations, DevOps automation, building Voice Agents, improving productivity, and securing AI Agent connections.

Unlike a regular theoretical session, the program focused on highly applicable topics connected to real-world problems in modern cloud environments. Through technical sharing sessions and demos, I better understood that Cloud is not only an infrastructure platform, but also an important environment for deploying intelligent AI systems that can analyze data, support decision-making, and automate workflows.

## General Information

* **Time:** 09:00 - 12:00, June 27, 2026
* **Location:** Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
* **Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day - June 2026
* **Theme:** Data Driven, AI Risen
* **Format:** Community event, knowledge sharing, technical demos, and networking
* **Role:** Participant

## Activities

* Joined the **Building Voice Agent at Scale** session and learned how Voice Agents can be designed from real-time audio processing to production agent runtime deployment.
* Learned more about **AWS DevOps Agent** and related learning resources, including ECS Workshop, Network Workshop, Best Practices, and foundational theory.
* Gained insight into how AWS Cloud supports scalable, maintainable, and enterprise-ready AI Agent systems.
* Connected with the First Cloud AI Journey community and broadened my perspective on Cloud, AI, and DevOps Automation.
